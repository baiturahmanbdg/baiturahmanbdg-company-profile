# **App Name**: Baiturahman Digital Hub

## Core Features:

- About Us Section: Displays the rich history and guiding mission of Masjid Baiturahman, presented in a clean and readable format.
- Daily Prayer Schedule: A dynamic display of daily prayer times, ensuring visitors have up-to-date information.
- Events & Activities List: Showcases upcoming community events, lectures, and activities with relevant dates and details.
- Contact & Location: Provides essential contact information for the mosque, complemented by an interactive map for easy navigation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A serene and grounding green (#559933) reflecting spirituality and growth, suitable for main headings and interactive elements.
- Background color: A subtly desaturated, light green (#F1F5F0) that offers a tranquil and clean canvas, evoking a peaceful ambiance.
- Accent color: A soft, radiant gold (#D6B85C) to highlight important information and add a touch of warmth and elegance, resonating with cultural significance.
- Headlines font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for its modern clarity and welcoming appeal, ensuring titles are inviting.
- Body text font: 'Alegreya' (serif) for enhanced readability in longer sections like 'About Us', offering an elegant and intellectual touch.
- Clean, line-based icons for navigation and informational elements, with subtle Islamic geometric patterns used sparingly for decoration.
- A responsive, single-page layout with clear section separators and ample white space to maintain a serene and uncluttered visual experience.
- Minimalistic animations such as gentle fade-ins for content sections and smooth scroll transitions between anchors to enhance user experience without distraction.
